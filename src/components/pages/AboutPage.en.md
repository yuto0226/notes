Here's how I'd describe myself:

I'm a deeply curious person, driven to understand how things work and where they come from. I can be a bit of a perfectionist about my work though, which means I often end up procrastinating until the last minute.

The version of myself I'm aiming for is mentally strong, knowledgeable, and full of life. I still want to make time to experience the world and keep growing stronger; that's part of why I wanted to present this blog the way I have. I hope to become a researcher someday, someone who can independently study how ideas developed and form original perspectives of my own.

I think that's also why I admire Himmel, an utterly ordinary man who became a hero without the Sword of the Hero's approval, simply through how he faced setbacks and his sheer persistence. I'm still moved by the line he left behind: 「ほんの少しでいい。誰かの人生を変えてあげればいい。きっとそれだけで十分なだ。」 ("Just a little is enough. If it changes even one person's life, I think that's more than enough.")
